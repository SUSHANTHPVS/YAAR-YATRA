Why Choose Yaar Yatra?

Yaar Yatra means 'Friend's Journey', and that's exactly what we help you plan—a journey built with companionship, trust, and intelligence. We go beyond simple bookings to offer truly personalized, crowdsourced travel planning.

Transparent Costing

Our unique Trip Calculator allows you to track expenses for every destination in real-time, helping you stick to your budget without surprises.

Real Community Feedback

Intelligent Recommendations

Always Prepared

About Yaar Yatra

Yaar Yatra was founded in 2024 by a team of enthusiastic travelers who realized that the best travel advice often comes from friends. We set out to build a platform that combines the intelligence of modern planning tools with the heart of a supportive travel community.

Our mission is to empower every traveler, whether you're planning a quick weekend getaway in India or a multi-country adventure abroad. We believe in transparency, authenticity, and making every Yatra unforgettable.

Happy Travels from the Yaar Yatra Team!

Privacy Policy

1. Data Collection and Usage

We collect your mobile number and chosen travel destination to facilitate OTP-based login and personalized travel planning. We use location and date data for personalized recommendations and weather integration. We do not share your private account details (like Aadhar or Admin registration data) with third parties. All booking history is stored locally (mocked via localStorage) for your convenience.

2. Security Measures

All authentication is secured via a one-time mock password (OTP). Admin data, though mocked, is treated with high confidentiality, ensuring each admin can only access bookings specific to their assigned location and current day.

Terms of Service

1. Booking Agreement

Yaar Yatra provides a travel planning and booking interface. All generated prices, bus/hotel assignments, and amenities are based on mock data for demonstration purposes. In a real-world scenario, actual costs and availability would apply. Confirmed bookings are non-refundable (mock scenario).

2. Content Usage

Users are solely responsible for the content of their posted reviews. We reserve the right to remove any content deemed offensive or inappropriate. By using this service, you agree that Yaar Yatra is not liable for any discrepancies arising from third-party links (maps, external data).
